Getting it onto your iPhone

  1. Keep the JPG and MOV together. They share a hidden ID that makes them one Live Photo.
  2. From a PC: open icloud.com/photos, click Upload, and select both files at once. They sync to your iPhone as a single Live Photo.
  3. From a Mac: drag both files into the Photos app, or AirDrop both to your iPhone.
  4. Saving the files on the iPhone through the Files app keeps them as a separate photo and video.
